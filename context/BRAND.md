# Brand (neutral default)

Replace this with your company's brand guide.

- **Colors:** background #0b0d10, text #f4f5f7, secondary text #9aa3ad, one accent #14b8a6. Never more than one accent per scene.
- **Type:** Inter (or the system sans-serif). Headlines 72–110 px, weight 700, tight tracking. Body 32–40 px.
- **Layout:** generous margins (at least 120 px at 1920 wide). One idea per scene. Text stays inside the safe area.
- **Motion:** short eased entrances (0.4–0.7 s, power3.out). No spinning, bouncing or flashing.
- **Tone:** clear and confident. Short sentences. No exclamation marks.
- **Endings:** finish on a simple end card with the product name and one call to action.
