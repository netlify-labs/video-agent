# Standard prompts for a look

Render these three with your look before you open a pull request, and attach a GIF of each (see README.md). They use
the fictional product Lumen, so nothing in them comes from the look's example (which is about Orbit).

## 1. A 16:9 launch (20 s)

> A 20-second launch video for Lumen, a shared notebook for small teams: notes, tasks and docs in one place. The
> news: instant search across everything you've written. End with "Try Lumen free".

## 2. A 9:16 social clip (15 s)

> A 15-second vertical social clip for Lumen announcing offline mode: write anywhere, and it syncs the moment you're
> back online.

## 3. A 30 s explainer with a chart (16:9)

> A 30-second explainer for Lumen's new team insights: how many notes, tasks and docs a team wrote each month this
> year (January 120, February 180, March 260, April 310, May 420, June 560), shown as a chart that builds. End with
> "See your team's insights in Lumen".
